Press Play to see the two factors and how their lengths and angles combine.
Multiply the lengths: 2 × 1.5 = 3
Add the angles: 20° + 60° = 80°
Therefore: z₁z₂ = 3 ∠ 80°

The red product vector is not the vector sum z₁ + z₂. It is constructed by multiplying the moduli and adding the arguments. The green vector shows the second factor's own length and angle; its 60° angle is the amount added to z₁'s 20° direction.
