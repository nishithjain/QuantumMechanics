The dot product and its geometric meaning

The same number can be calculated from coordinates or understood as a projection.

Interactive vector animation

Vector u

u = (5, 1)

Blue arrow: 5 right, 1 up

Vector v

v = (3, 7)

Red arrow: 3 right, 7 up

Dot product from coordinates

u · v = 5×3 + 1×7
= 15 + 7 = 22

Projection meaning

The green arrow is the part of u that points along v.

Its length is the scalar projection:

u · v / ‖v‖ = 22 / √58 ≈ 2.89

Press Play to build the picture: draw u and v, then drop the perpendicular projection of u onto v.
Geometric formula
u · v = ‖u‖ ‖v‖ cos θ
The dot product measures how much the vectors point in the same direction.
For this example
‖u‖ = √26, ‖v‖ = √58, and θ ≈ 55.5°.
√26 × √58 × cos(55.5°) ≈ 22

The green projection is a vector along v, not the perpendicular dashed line. The dashed line joins the tip of u to the projection tip and meets v at 90°. Because the angle is acute, the projection and dot product are positive.
