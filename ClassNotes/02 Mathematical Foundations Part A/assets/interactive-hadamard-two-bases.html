The same arrow can have different coordinate labels depending on which basis you use.

Change-of-basis cartoon

The rotated (Hadamard) basis

h+ = (1/√2)(1, 1)

h− = (1/√2)(1, −1)

Both have length 1 and are perpendicular.

Same vector, different coordinates

e1 in {e1, e2}: (1, 0)

e1 in {h+, h−}: (1/√2, 1/√2)

e2 in {h+, h−}: (1/√2, −1/√2)

Change of basis (both directions)

e1 = (h+ + h−)/√2

e2 = (h+ − h−)/√2

For v = (α, β):

coords in {h+, h−} =
((α+β)/√2, (α−β)/√2)
