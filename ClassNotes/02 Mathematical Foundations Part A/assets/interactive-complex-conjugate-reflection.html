Complex conjugate = reflection in the real axis

Watch the point for z = 3 + 2i reflect across the real axis to become its conjugate.

Press Play to see the complex number reflected across the real axis.
z = 3 + 2i   →   z̄ = 3 − 2i
The real part stays 3. The imaginary part changes sign: +2 becomes −2. The distance from the origin stays the same.
