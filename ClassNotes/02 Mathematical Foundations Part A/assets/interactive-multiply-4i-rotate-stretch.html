The product (4i)z can be understood as two simple operations, performed one after the other.

Press Play to follow the two operations: rotate first, then stretch.
Step 1 — Multiply by i (rotate): z → iz
Length stays 2; angle changes from 18° to 18° + 90° = 108°.
Step 2 — Multiply by 4 (stretch): iz → 4iz
Length changes from 2 to 2 × 4 = 8; angle stays 108°.
Final: (4i)z = 8ei3π/5, with length 8 and angle 108°.

The orange vector is the intermediate result after rotation by i. It has the same length as the blue vector. The green vector is four times longer but points in exactly the same direction as the orange vector. The red vector shows the multiplier 4i itself.
