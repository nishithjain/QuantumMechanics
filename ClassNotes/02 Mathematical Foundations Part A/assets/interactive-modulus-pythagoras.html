Modulus = distance from the origin

For z = 3 + 4i, watch how the real and imaginary parts form a right triangle.

Press Play to build the right triangle and calculate the modulus.
|z|² = a² + b² = 3² + 4² = 9 + 16 = 25
|z| = √25 = 5
The modulus (also called magnitude or absolute value) is the length of the vector from the origin to the point (3, 4).
