Adding vectors and scaling a vector

Two different operations: add corresponding coordinates, or multiply every coordinate by a scalar.

Interactive vector animation

1. Addition: add coordinates

2. Scaling: multiply every coordinate

Press Play to explore vector addition and scalar multiplication.

Addition: add matching coordinates

u = (4, 1)
v = (1, 3)
u + v = (4 + 1, 1 + 3) = (5, 4)

Scaling: multiply every coordinate

v = (2, 1)
2v = (2×2, 2×1) = (4, 2)
−v = (−1×2, −1×1) = (−2, −1)

A positive scalar stretches or shrinks a vector without changing its direction. A negative scalar also reverses its direction (a 180° flip). Vector addition is different: add the x-coordinates together and the y-coordinates together.
