Z-basis probabilities

Each T adds 45° of relative phase to the |1⟩ amplitude. Two T gates give S, four give Z, and eight give a full turn. The π/8 nickname refers to the half-angle in the Rz matrix. T itself turns the Bloch vector by π/4.
