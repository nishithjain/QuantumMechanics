Try the measurement yourself

State (|00⟩+|01⟩+|10⟩)/√3 — choose the outcome of measuring only the first qubit.

Probability: 2/3

Remaining basis states:

|00⟩|01⟩

Normalized final state:

(|00⟩ + |01⟩) / √2

In words: The first qubit is definitely 0; the second qubit is still in an equal superposition of 0 and 1.

Probability: 1/3

Remaining basis states:

|10⟩

Normalized final state:

|10⟩

In words: The first qubit is definitely 1; the second qubit is definitely 0.

Tip: Compare with measuring both qubits — then each of |00⟩, |01⟩, and |10⟩ has probability 1/3 individually.
