What this teaches

Fidelity is |⟨ideal|noisy⟩|² for each pure-state trial. This models random X/Z gate faults; it does not by itself simulate amplitude damping, measurement noise, or all physical decoherence.

  • Change p and the random seed; multiple trials may produce different outputs.
  • A Z phase flip on |0⟩ is harmless, while X on |0⟩ is maximally damaging.
