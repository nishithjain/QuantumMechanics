What this teaches

The positions are computed from actual complex qubit amplitudes, not decorative dots. H+S reaches only six axis directions from |0⟩; adding T enables more locations as words get longer.

  • Use 9 for the maximum length to see the orbit become denser.
  • The distinct counts depend on exactly which gate alphabet and maximum length you enumerate.
  • Two different gate strings can produce the same physical state.
