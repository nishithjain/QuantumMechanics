1. Why do we calculate det(Y − λI) = 0?

A zero determinant ensures that (Y − λI)v = 0 has a nonzero solution. Solving the characteristic equation gives us the eigenvalues.

2. What is i × i?

By definition, i² = −1. Therefore i × i = −1.

3. For λ = +1, what eigenvector did we find?

For λ = +1, we solve Yv = v. Choosing v₁ = 1 gives v₂ = i. Therefore the eigenvector is (1, i)ᵀ.

4. Why do we divide the eigenvector by √2?

The squared magnitudes of 1 and i add up to 2. Dividing the vector by √2 makes the total probability equal to 1.
