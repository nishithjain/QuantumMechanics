What to watch for
This ideal circuit works for any normalized α and β. The two classical bits specify only a known correction; they do not reveal the amplitudes.
  • Select every measurement result: 00 → I; 01 → X; 10 → Z; 11 → X then Z.
  • The same correction rule works with an imaginary relative phase.
  • A Bell pair is consumed, and Alice’s original qubit is not left as a clone.
