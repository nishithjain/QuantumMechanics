What to watch for
This is the decoder used in both teleportation and superdense coding. Select every state and predict the final bits before pressing Play.
  • The Bell decoder is CNOT first, then H on the first qubit.
  • The final two digits refer to top and bottom wires in this order.
  • Changing only the sign changes the first measured bit; switching Φ and Ψ changes the second.
