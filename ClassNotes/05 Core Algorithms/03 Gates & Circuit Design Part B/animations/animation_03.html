What to watch for
The toy input is |ψ⟩=|0⟩ and |φ⟩=cosθ|0⟩+sinθ|1⟩. The copying proof requires z=z². Only θ=0° or θ=90° can satisfy it.
  • At 0°, the two states are identical and z=1.
  • At 90°, the two states are orthogonal and z=0.
  • At 45°, z≈0.7071 but z²=0.5: the proof reaches a contradiction.
