What to watch for
A test with a definite right answer is better than checking whether a teleported plus state happens to look 50–50. Skip the correction to see the important no-signalling result.
  • Try θ=0°, 60°, 90°, and 180°; after correct teleport–undo, P(0)=1 every time.
  • With corrections skipped and Alice’s bits unknown, Bob is 50–50 for every θ.
  • The inverse rotation is a verification device, not a necessary step in quantum teleportation itself.
