Watch the three basis vectors rotate

The lecture's transformation rotates space 90° around the y-axis. The y-direction stays fixed; the x- and z-directions change.

î (x direction) ĵ (y direction) k̂ (z direction)
Start: î=(1,0,0), ĵ=(0,1,0), k̂=(0,0,1)
Diagram is a clear 2D projection of 3D axes. The controls animate the basis directions toward their lecture-defined destinations.
What to notice
  • ĵ remains on the y-axis because the rotation is around that axis.
  • î ends at (0,0,-1), the negative z direction.
  • k̂ ends at (1,0,0), the positive x direction.
