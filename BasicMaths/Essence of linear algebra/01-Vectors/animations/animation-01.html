Animation 01 — Coordinates as walking instructions

In the lecture, a 2D vector is an arrow from the origin. Its first number tells us how far to go horizontally, and its second number tells us how far to go vertically. Change the coordinates, then click Step to follow the route.

v = [3, 2]

Try x = −2, y = 3, matching the negative-coordinate picture in the notes. Negative x means left; negative y means down. The final arrow does not depend on the order in which you imagine taking the horizontal and vertical steps.
