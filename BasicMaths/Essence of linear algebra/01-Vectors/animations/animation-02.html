Animation 02 — Adding two vectors

The lecture’s central example is u = [1, 2] and v = [3, −1]. Their sum is the combined journey. Watch the second arrow move to the tip of the first one, then follow the new arrow from the origin to the endpoint.

Remember: moving v to another location on the diagram does not change its length or direction. The orange arrow is not a third journey: it is the single displacement equivalent to doing both journeys.
