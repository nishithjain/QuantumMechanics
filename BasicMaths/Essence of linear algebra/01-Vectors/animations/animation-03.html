Animation 03 — Stretch, shrink, and reverse

A scalar is a number multiplying a vector. In the lecture, multiplying by 2 stretches, multiplying by ⅓ shrinks, and multiplying by −1.8 reverses the arrow and stretches it. Watch the dark arrow change while the pale blue starting arrow stays fixed.

2.0
v = [1, 2]
k · v = [2, 4]

The pale blue arrow is always the original [1, 2]. The dark red arrow is the current multiple of the original. A negative multiplier turns the arrow through 180°; zero collapses it to the origin.
