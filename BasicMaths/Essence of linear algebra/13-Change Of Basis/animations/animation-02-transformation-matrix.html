Why does a transformation become A⁻¹MA?

We want the same geometric transformation expressed in Jennifer's coordinates. First translate into standard coordinates, apply M, then translate back.

Standard transformation matrix M

Remember the order
  1. A: Jennifer → standard.
  2. M: perform the transformation in standard coordinates.
  3. A⁻¹: standard → Jennifer.

The rightmost matrix acts first, so the complete formula is MJ = A⁻¹ M A.
