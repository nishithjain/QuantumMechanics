Same point, two coordinate systems

The white dot is always P. Watch how two sets of directions take you to it from the same origin.

Mint · your route   Gold · Jennifer’s route

Your coordinates (3, 2)

î = (1, 0) and ĵ = (0, 1)

Go 3 units right, then 2 units up.

P = 3î + 2ĵ

Jennifer’s coordinates (5/3, 1/3)J

b₁ = (2, 1) and b₂ = (−1, 1)

Go 5/3 times along b₁, then 1/3 times along b₂. That second leg moves left and up.

P = (5/3)b₁ + (1/3)b₂

Where the fractions come from

a(2, 1) + b(−1, 1) = (3, 2)

  1. Match x and y to get 2a − b = 3 and a + b = 2.
  2. Add the equations. The b terms cancel, leaving 3a = 5, so a = 5/3.
  3. Substitute into the second equation to get b = 2 − 5/3 = 1/3.
