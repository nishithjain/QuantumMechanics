Play a transformation forward, then reverse it

Here, A is a 90° counterclockwise rotation. Its inverse A⁻¹ is a 90° clockwise rotation. The inverse takes the transformed vector back to the original vector.

A = [[0, −1], [1, 0]]   A⁻¹ = [[0, 1], [−1, 0]]   A⁻¹A = I

Original vector: (2, 1)

Remember: The inverse is not an entry-by-entry reciprocal. It is the transformation that undoes A.
