What does the determinant tell us?

Watch the same unit square change under three linear transformations. The determinant tells us the factor by which area changes.

1. Determinant = 3 — area becomes three times larger

The square stretches horizontally by a factor of 3. Its height stays 1, so the new area is 3 × 1 = 3.

The pictures use simple example matrices: diag(3,1), diag(½,1), and diag(1,0). They illustrate the area effect; many different matrices can have the same determinant.
