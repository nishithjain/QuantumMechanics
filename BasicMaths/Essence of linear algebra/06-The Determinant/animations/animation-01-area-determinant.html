Explore a 2D determinant

Each matrix column tells where one basis vector goes. The transformed unit square becomes a parallelogram. Its area is |det(A)|; the determinant's sign tells whether orientation is reversed.

How to read it
  • Blue arrow: transformed î, the first matrix column.
  • Green arrow: transformed ĵ, the second matrix column.
  • Shaded parallelogram: transformed unit square.
  • Area magnitude = |det(A)|. Negative determinant means orientation reversal.
