From a unit cube to a parallelepiped

In 3D, the determinant measures volume scaling. This model uses an easy-to-read family of transformations: stretch each coordinate direction, with an optional shear. The resulting parallelepiped's volume magnitude is the absolute determinant.

Notice
  • A unit cube starts with volume 1.
  • Shearing slants the cube but does not necessarily change its volume.
  • If any scale factor becomes zero in this model, the solid becomes flat and its volume becomes zero.
  • A negative product of the three scale factors reverses orientation.
