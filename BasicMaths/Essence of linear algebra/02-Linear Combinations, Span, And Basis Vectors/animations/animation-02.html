2. What can two vectors reach? The span

Change the type of starting vectors, then turn the two scalar “knobs.” A span is every result you could ever obtain when the knobs are allowed to vary freely.

a × ub × wResultAvailable destinations (span)
“Play” holds a fixed while b changes from −3 to +3.

Key idea: Even though you are seeing one arrow at a time, the faint blue background or purple line indicates all results reachable by arbitrary choices of a and b. In 2D the options are plane, line, or origin.
