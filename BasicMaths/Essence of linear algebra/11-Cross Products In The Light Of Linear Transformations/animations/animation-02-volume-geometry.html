Why the dual vector is the cross product

Think of v and w as the base of a parallelogram. The signed volume made with r is base area × signed perpendicular height. The vector p = v × w points perpendicular to the base and has length equal to its area.

Key insight

The base area is |v × w|. The signed perpendicular height is the component of r along the correctly oriented unit normal. Multiplying them gives the signed volume, which is also (v × w) · r.
