The cross product is the dual vector

Fix vectors v and w. The function T(r) = det[r v w] returns signed volume. Duality says there is a vector p such that T(r) = p · r. Watch both calculations agree.

What to notice

The determinant T(r) and the dot product p · r always give the same number. The vector p is the dual vector of T — and its coordinates are exactly those of v × w.
