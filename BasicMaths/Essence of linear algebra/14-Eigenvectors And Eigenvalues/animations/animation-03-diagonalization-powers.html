Why an eigenbasis makes powers easier

Use the lecture's diagonal example: one basis direction is scaled by −1 and the other by 2. In the eigenbasis, the matrix is diagonal. Repeating the transformation simply raises each eigenvalue to the chosen power.

Change of basis reminder

If P contains independent eigenvectors as columns and M is the original matrix, then D = P⁻¹MP. After computing Dⁿ, convert back: Mⁿ = PDⁿP⁻¹.

The animation uses D directly so the simple repeated-scaling behavior is easy to see.
