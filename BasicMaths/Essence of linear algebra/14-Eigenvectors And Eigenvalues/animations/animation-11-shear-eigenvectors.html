What the shear does

S = 1101   S(x, y) = (x + y, y)

Find the eigenvalue

S − λI = 1 − λ101 − λ

det(S − λI) = (1 − λ)²

This is zero only at λ = 1. The same root occurs twice.

Find the eigenvectors

S − I = 0100

(S − I)(x, y) = (y, 0)

To get (0, 0), we need y = 0. Every nonzero arrow on the x-axis stays fixed. These arrows are all multiples of (1, 0), so they supply only one independent direction. Two independent directions would be needed to span the plane.
