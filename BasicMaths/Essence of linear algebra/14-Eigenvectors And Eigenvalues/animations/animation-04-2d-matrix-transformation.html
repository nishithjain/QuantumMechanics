How a matrix transforms a 2D plane

Watch the two standard basis vectors move. Their destinations become the columns of the matrix, and the whole grid follows the same transformation.

Light dashed lines = original square grid behind the motion. Solid grid = current transformation. Blue = original î and ĵ. Orange = transformed î. Green = transformed ĵ. Purple = example vector.
