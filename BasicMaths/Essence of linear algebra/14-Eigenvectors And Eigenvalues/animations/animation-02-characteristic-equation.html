Find eigenvalues by making the determinant zero

Enter a 2×2 matrix A = [[a,b],[c,d]]. The calculator forms det(A − λI), solves the quadratic, and reports whether the eigenvalues are real or complex. This demonstrates the characteristic-equation step from the lecture.

Next step after finding λ

For each eigenvalue, substitute it into (A − λI)v = 0 and solve the resulting homogeneous linear system. The solutions other than v = 0 are the eigenvectors associated with that eigenvalue.
