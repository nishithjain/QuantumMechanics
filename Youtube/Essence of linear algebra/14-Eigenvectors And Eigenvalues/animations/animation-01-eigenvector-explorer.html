Which vectors stay on their own line?

Explore the lecture's matrix A = [[3, 1], [0, 2]]. Choose an input vector. The blue arrow is the input; the red arrow is its transformed result. An eigenvector's two arrows lie on the same line.

What makes a vector an eigenvector?

For input v = (x, y), the transformation gives Av = (3x + y, 2y). It is an eigenvector when Av is a scalar multiple of v. In 2D, that means the two arrows lie on the same line through the origin.
